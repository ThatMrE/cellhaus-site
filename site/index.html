The whole process

Start fast in Kobe. Grow into trials. Reach the market.

Stage 1 · Months 0–6 · Start fast

A · Join a clinic plan

A Kobe clinic already has a plan. Weeks. Low control. Data often not shared.

B · CellHaus plan

A sponsor files a new single-patient plan for your design. Months. Open data.

Stage 2 · Months 6–18

A small series

More patients under the same law in certified clinics. Unchain tracks safety and results.

Stage 3 · Year 2+

Run trials

Use the human data to design a trial. In Japan under the drug law, in the US with an FDA IND, or both.

Stage 4 · Approval

Reach the market

Conditional approval in Japan (up to 7 years), or full FDA approval (usually 7 to 12 years).

The founder keeps all rights at every stage. Early human data can help design a trial. It does not replace one.

Rules that protect you

Fast must never mean unsafe.

Independent check. The intake doctor gets no money from your choice.

No plan, no payment. You pay nothing for treatment until a committee reviews the plan and the doctor files it.

Register first. Each case goes into the registry before the first dose.

Publish everything. Good and bad results both go public.

Trials first. If a trial fits you, we tell you, even if it is not in Kobe.

Home care stays. Your home doctor is part of the plan from day one.
