Privacy · 個人情報

Your data, your story, your choice.

How CellHaus handles the information you share when you apply. It includes health information, so we keep it short and clear.

Last updated 24 October 2026.

Draft policy

This policy is under legal review and may change before launch.

The short version

  • We use your application to review your fit for the program. Nothing else.
  • We never sell your data or use it for ads.
  • An independent doctor and the program team see it. A hospital sees it only if you agree.
  • Email us any time to see, fix or delete your data.

1 · Who we are

The CellHaus Accelerator is a Haus program in Kobe, Japan. We run it with Mirai Tech City and Unchain Bio.

When this policy says "we" or "CellHaus", it means the people who run the program. CellHaus is not a clinic and does not give medical care.

Questions about your data go to elliot@haus.fund.

2 · What we collect

When you apply, we collect what you type into the form:

  • Your role: patient-founder, parent founder, early-stage company, or family member or guide.
  • Your name, email, country and company name.
  • Your disease or condition, therapy type, stage and when you hope to start.
  • Which records and data you already have, such as medical records or sequence files.
  • Your story, in your own words.

Some of this is health information. The law treats it as sensitive, and so do we.

Please do not paste full medical records, scans or genetic data into the form. If we move forward, we will agree with you how to share files safely.

3 · Why we use it

We use your information to:

  • Review your application and decide if the program fits you.
  • Let an independent intake doctor check whether your case could be suitable, or whether a clinical trial is a better option.
  • Contact you about your application and the cohort.
  • Plan the program if you join.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising.

We use your information because you give us your consent when you apply. You can withdraw it at any time. See section 7.

4 · Who sees it

  • The CellHaus team, including staff at Mirai Tech City and Unchain Bio who run the program.
  • The independent intake doctor who reviews applications.
  • A partner academic hospital in Kobe, only if you move forward and agree first.
  • Service providers who help us run the site: Netlify hosts this website and stores form submissions, and our email provider delivers messages to us.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your application is not published. The open registry mentioned on the form is for treatment results from CellHaus tracks. We will ask for separate consent before anything about you goes into it.

5 · Where it is stored

Netlify stores form submissions on servers in the United States. People who review applications may work in Japan, the United States or other countries.

This means your information may move across borders. We only share it with people who need it for the purposes above. If you are in Japan, section 11 explains what this means under Japanese law.

6 · How long we keep it

If you do not join the program, we delete your application within 24 months of the cohort decision.

If you join, we keep your information for as long as you take part, and as long as the law requires after that.

You can ask us to delete it sooner. See section 7.

7 · Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the information we hold about you.
  • Correct it.
  • Delete it.
  • Stop using it, by withdrawing your consent.

Email elliot@haus.fund. We reply within 30 days. Withdrawing consent ends your application, but it does not undo what we did before.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in Japan, see section 11.

8 · Applying for someone else

Parents and guides often apply for someone they care for. If you apply about another person, you must have their permission, or be their parent or legal guardian.

If the person is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must submit the application.

9 · Security

The site uses HTTPS, so the form is encrypted on its way to us. Only people who need to review applications can see them.

No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong that affects your information, we will tell you.

10 · Cookies and tracking

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Our fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads. Netlify keeps short-term server logs for security.

11 · If you are in Japan: the APPI

This section explains how we follow Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (個人情報の保護に関する法律, the "APPI"). It applies because the program runs in Kobe. It adds to the rest of this policy.

Health information needs your consent first

Under the APPI, information about your disease, medical history and treatment is "special care-required personal information" (要配慮個人情報). We only collect it with your consent. You give that consent when you tick the data consent box on the form and send it.

We never share this information on an opt-out basis.

Purposes of use

We use your personal information only for the purposes in section 3. If we want to use it for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

Sharing with others in Japan

We share your information with the people in section 4, such as the independent intake doctor and Mirai Tech City and Unchain Bio staff who run the program. We do this based on your consent.

We share it with a partner academic hospital in Kobe only if you move forward and agree at that time.

When we share information with someone else, we keep a record of it, as the APPI requires.

Sending your information outside Japan

Your application is stored by Netlify, Inc. in the United States. Some reviewers may also work outside Japan. Before you send the form, you should know:

  • Country: the United States of America.
  • The rules there: the United States has no single national privacy law like the APPI. Protection comes from sector laws and some state laws. Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission has not found the United States to have an equivalent system.
  • What Netlify does: Netlify handles submissions for us under its terms and data processing addendum. These limit how it may use the data and require security measures.

You agree to this transfer when you tick the data consent box and send the form. If a reviewer outside the United States or Japan needs your information, we will tell you the country and ask you first.

How we keep it safe

We take security control measures (安全管理措置) to protect your information:

  • Only people who need to review applications can access them.
  • Access uses personal accounts, not shared passwords.
  • Data moves over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Anyone who reviews applications agrees to keep them confidential.

If information about you leaks, or might have leaked, we will report it to the Personal Information Protection Commission and tell you, as the APPI requires.

Your requests under the APPI

You can ask us to disclose (開示), correct, add to or delete (訂正・追加・削除), or stop using or sharing (利用停止・第三者提供の停止) the information we hold about you. You can also ask for our records of who we shared it with.

Email elliot@haus.fund with your name and what you want us to do. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We reply without delay, and within 30 days. We do not charge a fee. You can write in English or Japanese.

Complaints

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you already gave us, we will email you.
